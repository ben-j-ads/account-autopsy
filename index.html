    How it works

    The score

    Twenty yes or no questions in four groups of five: measurement, structure, creative, and budget and strategy. Each yes is one point. Fix-first runs in the order shown, because a gap in measurement makes every later answer harder to trust.

    solid: 17 or more. patchy: 12 to 16. leaky: 7 to 11. flying blind: 6 or fewer. The bands are this tool's choice.

    Read this before you trust the grade

    This is a checklist of good practice, not a validated test. The questions are the author's judgement, drawn from effectiveness research and platform guidance. A high score does not make an account profitable, and a low one does not make it unprofitable.

    It grades the setup, not the results. Results come from your product, price and market as much as from how the account is run.

    Some questions are platform-specific. The learning-phase question follows Meta's published guidance. Skip it, and subtract one from the bar, if you do not run Meta.
