# Account Autopsy

Twenty yes or no questions that grade how a paid social account is set up: measurement, structure, creative, and budget and strategy. Tick what is true today, get a score, the weakest group, and the three things to fix first.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step and no dependencies.

- **Verdict:** `solid` (17+), `patchy` (12 to 16), `leaky` (7 to 11) or `flying blind` (6 or fewer). The bands are this tool's choice.
- **Shows:** the score, a bar per group, and the first three unticked questions in order, with a one-line reason each. Measurement comes first on purpose.
- **Share:** a paper-ground PNG card, and the URL hash carries your ticks.

A checklist of good practice, not a validated instrument. It grades the setup, not the results. The learning-phase question follows Meta's published guidance and can be skipped if you do not run Meta.
